The homepage category section uses the site's dark navy palette and single purple accent. Categories appear in one horizontal, swipeable row on desktop and mobile, with tall game-cover imagery, restrained angled clipping, and prominent names below. Use Libre Baskerville for category names and IBM Plex Sans Arabic as the fallback. Preserve real category data and links.
